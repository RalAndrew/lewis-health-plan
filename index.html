Household Kitchen Strategy

🏠 The Golden Rule — One Pot, Two Portions

Cook one base meal for both of you each day. Then each person adds their specific needs at the plate level. This eliminates double cooking, reduces cost, and keeps the household running efficiently. Almost everything on both plans overlaps — the difference is quantity (Rolando eats more) and additions (Stephanie adds seeds and specific supplements).

🟢 Rolando's Plate Rules

  • Larger portions of starchy carbs — breadfruit, dasheen, sweet potato
  • 3 eggs where Stephanie has 2
  • Add peanut butter to snacks for calorie surplus
  • Coconut milk in oats and rice for extra kcal
  • Post-workout meal within 45 min of training
  • Total daily target: 2,500–2,800 kcal

🟣 Stephanie's Plate Rules

  • Smaller, balanced portions — not a surplus
  • Flaxseeds on oats, smoothies, and salads daily
  • Pumpkin seeds and walnuts as snacks
  • ALWAYS cook cruciferous veg — never raw
  • Cook with coconut oil or olive oil only
  • Finish dinner 2–3 hrs before bed
  • Sit upright 20 min after eating

✅ Foods That Serve Both Goals Simultaneously

  • Oily fish (snapper, mahi-mahi, tuna) BOTH — Omega-3 for Rolando's thyroid + anti-inflammatory for Stephanie's endometriosis
  • Eggs BOTH — Protein for Rolando's muscle building + choline for Stephanie's egg quality
  • Avocado BOTH — Calorie density for Rolando + healthy fats for Stephanie's hormone health
  • Callalou BOTH — Iron and folate for both; always steam or sauté
  • Sweet potato, dasheen, ground provisions BOTH — Complex carbs with steady glycaemic response
  • Legumes (kidney beans, lentils, chickpeas, black-eyed peas) BOTH — Plant protein, fibre, hormone support
  • Turmeric + ginger + garlic in all cooking BOTH — Anti-inflammatory base for both
  • Coconut water BOTH — Hydration and electrolytes for both
  • Pumpkin and okra BOTH — Blood sugar regulation and anti-inflammatory
  • Brown rice BOTH — Stable carb base; Rolando gets larger portion

⚠️ Key Differences to Manage at the Plate

  • Peanut butter Rolando — Stephanie can have small amounts but not as a daily calorie booster
  • Breadfruit (large portions) Rolando — Stephanie has smaller portions of starchy sides
  • Flaxseeds (1 tbsp daily) Stephanie — Lignans support estrogen metabolism; add to her oats/smoothie
  • Walnuts Stephanie — Plant omega-3 and hormone support; Rolando uses peanuts instead
  • Pumpkin seeds daily Stephanie — Zinc for FSH production; Rolando uses sunflower seeds
  • Fermented foods Stephanie — Gut microbiome supports estrogen clearance; kefir or natural yoghurt if available
  • No gluten/dairy/sugar during period Stephanie — Strict protocol during menstrual phase; Rolando continues normally
  • Moringa tea at bedtime Stephanie — Anti-inflammatory; Rolando has soursop or guava leaf tea

🍳 Weekly Meal Prep Strategy (Sunday)

  • Batch cook legumes: Cook a large pot of kidney beans or lentils — used across Mon–Wed for both
  • Prep ground provisions: Boil/roast a tray of dasheen, sweet potato, breadfruit — store in fridge, reheat as needed
  • Make oat energy balls: Oats + peanut butter + banana — Rolando's pre-workout snacks for the week
  • Batch cook brown rice: One large pot serves 3–4 days
  • Marinate fish/chicken: Use lime, garlic, turmeric, thyme — season in bulk, freeze portions
  • Make herbal teas in bulk: Soursop, guava leaf, ginger — store in fridge as cold brew or reheat
  • Stephanie: Prep flaxseeds in a jar — pre-measure weekly portions; soak chia if using

📅 Stephanie — Cycle Phase Protocol

  • Follicular (Day 1–14): More energy available — slightly more active movement. Evening primrose oil in this phase only. Good time to batch cook and meal prep.
  • Luteal (Day 15–28): Honour lower energy. Increase magnesium dose. Stricter diet — no gluten, refined sugar, dairy. Start mefenamic acid 1–2 days before period if prescribed.
  • During period: Rest is medicine. Strict diet only. Ginger and turmeric tea frequently. Skip NAC on period days. Heat therapy daily. Legs up the wall every evening.
